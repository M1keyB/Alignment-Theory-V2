# Capacity-Forming Functions Terminology

## Canonical Term

The canonical Alignment Theory term is **capacity-forming functions**.

Canonical definition:

> Capacity-forming functions are activities that people, groups, or systems must continue to perform in order to retain the ability to act, judge, adapt, and remain responsible.

Plain-language formulation:

> Capacity declines when people stop participating in the functions that develop and maintain their ability to act.

## HAPI-Specific Term

Use **agency-forming functions** where the surrounding text is specifically about HAPI, human agency preservation, refusal, human judgment, accountability, or the development of human agency.

## Plain-Language Fallback

Use **essential functions** when the formal term would add weight without adding precision.

## Why Retire Load-Bearing As The Default

`Load-bearing` can be useful in literal architecture or engineering contexts, but as a public-facing theory metaphor it often asks readers to infer the mechanism. Alignment Theory should usually name the mechanism directly: a function forms or preserves capacity, supports participation, or substitutes for it.

## Permitted Retained Uses

Retain `load-bearing` only when it appears in:

- filenames
- URLs
- direct quotations
- citation titles
- historical audit excerpts
- PDF metadata that is not being regenerated
- code identifiers that preserve route or glossary compatibility
- literal engineering descriptions

## Correct Usage

- Capacity-forming functions are activities that must remain practiced if capacity is to remain alive.
- A support relation can preserve participation in a capacity-forming function.
- HAPI asks whether agency-forming functions such as judgment, refusal, revision, and accountability remain available to the person.
- The old URL may remain `load-bearing-function-participatory-capacity-and-the-four-modes-of-support.html` for compatibility while the visible page title changes.

## Wording To Avoid

- load-bearing functions
- load-bearing human capacities
- load-bearing structure
- load-bearing role
- load-bearing layer
- load-bearing truth
- load bearing
