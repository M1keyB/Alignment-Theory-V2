## Overall Claim Pattern

The PCPI page is careful in its limitations section. The main review pressure is not the formula itself, but the surrounding status language: metric, measurement framework, v1/v5, MAI, certification, validation, and whether examples are clearly illustrative.

## Claims That Should Not Be Strengthened Yet

- PCPI is validated.
- PCPI predicts retained skill over time.
- PCPI has proven inter-rater reliability.
- MAI is a public benchmark with reviewed access procedures.
- PCPI certification is operationally mature.
- PCPI applies unchanged to HAPI institutional audits.
