# PCPI Terminology Mapping

Phase 12 audit-only document. This maps current PCPI terms against newer Alignment Theory, HAPI, and applied-governance language without forcing later HAPI terms into PCPI.

## Summary

PCPI overlaps most strongly with Alignment Theory through participatory capacity, support, substitution, dependency, judgment, verification, and learning. It overlaps with HAPI through agency preservation, agency erosion, refusal, real authority, false gates, and dependency capture. The models remain distinct: PCPI currently scores AI prompt-output pairs, while HAPI addresses agency preservation across institutions, civic systems, and governance practices.

## Distinct Model Boundaries

PCPI:

- evaluates AI assistance at prompt-output and batch level
- uses a numerical scoring formula
- includes feature scores and penalty scores
- is currently best framed as a proposed measurement framework plus starter evaluator

HAPI:

- frames agency preservation across systems and institutions
- focuses on false gates, live authority, refusal, audit, restoration, and dependency capture
- may later use PCPI as one technical measurement tool
- should not absorb PCPI without author approval
