# PCPI HAPI Cross-Link Decision

Phase 12 audit-only document. No links were added.

## Conceptual Relationship

PCPI and HAPI overlap around agency preservation:

- PCPI scores whether AI assistance preserves or erodes user participation.
- HAPI frames human agency preservation across systems, institutions, audit practices, refusal, live authority, restoration, and dependency capture.

The overlap is real, but the models are not identical.

## Practical Relationship

PCPI is currently practical at the prompt-output or batch evaluation level. It includes a formula, features, score bands, examples, dataset template, and a starter Python evaluator.

HAPI is broader. It can include institutional, civic, governance, education, healthcare, religious, organizational, and public-system contexts. HAPI may use PCPI as one measurement tool when the object being evaluated is AI assistance or a prompt-output workflow.

## Terminology Overlap

Shared or adjacent terms:

- human agency
- agency preservation
- agency erosion
- dependency
- substitution
- participation
- refusal
- authority
- audit
- verification

Distinct terms:

- PCPI uses feature scoring, penalty scoring, classification bands, and prompt-output examples.
- HAPI uses agency theater, dependency capture, false gates, true gates, meaningful refusal, live human authority, and restoration language.

## Reader Confusion Risk

Risks if linked too early:

- readers may think PCPI is the full HAPI audit model
- readers may think HAPI has adopted PCPI thresholds
- readers may treat PCPI as validated certification infrastructure
- readers may apply prompt-output scoring to institutional contexts without a HAPI-specific rubric

Risk can be reduced with status wording.

## Recommended HAPI Link Placement

Add later under a small related-methods section, not in the hero:

- possible section: `Related research and measurement tools`
- possible anchor: near the AI alignment research link already present on HAPI
- status wording should say PCPI is a proposed measurement framework for AI assistance, not the full HAPI audit model

## Recommended PCPI Backlink Placement

Add later near `Related Research` or after `Validation Status and Limitations`:

- link to HAPI as a broader agency-preservation branch
- state that HAPI addresses institutional and civic agency preservation while PCPI scores AI assistance cases

## Recommendation

Recommendation: add after PCPI terminology revision.

Reason:

- HAPI already links to PCPI in current public HTML, but a controlled pass should decide whether that link remains, moves, or gains status wording.
- PCPI still has naming/version/maturity questions.
- A clean cross-link should distinguish PCPI from HAPI before adding stronger relationship language.

Do not add new links during Phase 12.
