Coherent agency under constraint

Read downward from reality and constraints through the functions and participation that maintain capacity. Agency requires that participation remain usable. Authority, governance, and continuity concern who may bind action and what must survive as it passes through systems and time.

AGS: the ten-node operator trajectory

The trajectory groups ten positions into five planes. Only AAG and Runtime Binding occupy the enforcement plane. The other positions establish human authority, deliberate, preserve evidence, support maturation, or expose the path to an operator.

Human-authority plane

  1. Human AgencyHuman understanding, judgment, refusal, responsibility, and direction.
  2. Governance SubstratePolicies, context, roles, and explicit authority boundaries.

Deliberative plane

  1. Context AdmissionThe place for deciding what context is admissible for reasoning.
  2. Agent ReasoningProposal formation under delegated scope.
  3. PGDLPre-Gate Deliberation Layer: scrutinizes and matures proposals; does not approve or execute.

Enforcement plane

  1. AAGAgent Action Gate: allows, requires approval, revises, or blocks before execution.
  2. Runtime BindingChecks that actual execution matches a valid permit.

Evidence and maturation plane

  1. ReceiptsTamper-evident evidence of the governance path; evidence is not approval.
  2. Governance MemoryAnalyzes receipt history and recommends improvements for human review.

Operator surface

  1. Continuity ConsoleMakes the trajectory and evidence visible to an operator.

Handoff Integrity crosses the whole trajectory: source lineage, validated context, authority scope, transformation history, expiration conditions, and proof must survive each transfer.

Semantic continuity: did meaning survive the handoff? Runtime continuity: did the authorized branch remain the executed branch?

This is operator-facing architecture from the canonical handoff. See AGS status and limitations for the retained implementation evidence; the map does not establish that every position is a separately implemented module.

Governance that develops capacity

Internalization links governance to future participation: initially external constraints become increasingly carried through understanding, skill, judgment, and governed habit. Unnecessary gating can become lighter where capacity and accountability mature.

In AGS, the loop runs from action to receipt, pattern detection, recommendation, human review, approved governance change, and improved future proposals. Governance Memory does not silently rewrite policy or authority. HAPI's maturity model describes the human and institutional application.

PCPI remains an early evaluation tool for participation in AI assistance. It does not measure or validate the entire theory shown here.
