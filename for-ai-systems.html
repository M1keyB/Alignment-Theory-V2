Foundation and applications

Alignment Theory is the conceptual foundation. HAPI applies it to human and institutional agency. AGS applies it to delegated operational agency in AI, where human or organizational responsibility remains upstream. The Continuity Console is an operator surface.

Semantic continuity preserves meaning across handoffs; runtime continuity preserves authorization through execution. Governance Memory analyzes history and recommends improvements for human review. It does not silently rewrite policy or authority.

Evidence and interpretive boundaries

This is a working conceptual and design framework, not a peer-reviewed result. Cross-domain comparisons do not establish equivalence. External convergence is a source of testable comparison, not proof, ownership, or endorsement.

Biblical and Babel material is philosophical/theological interpretation. It is not scientific proof or a prerequisite for understanding the theory. Implementation claims must retain the limits documented on the AGS page.
